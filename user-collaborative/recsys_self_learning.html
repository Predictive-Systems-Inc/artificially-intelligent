Collaborative filtering: borrow the opinions of people like Alice

No genres, no plots. Only the ratings matrix. First we measure who rates like Alice, then we let those people vote on the movies she has not seen.

1

Compare only the movies both rated: Mad Max, John Wick and The Notebook. Put Alice's and Bob's rating for the same movie in each pair. The page does the arithmetic.

(5 × 5) + (4 × 5) + (1 × 2) = 25 + 20 + 2 = 47. A bigger dot product means the two lists of ratings line up more.
2

Length of each rating vector

Each person's length uses only their own ratings on the shared movies.

|Bob| = √( ² + ² + ² ) = ?
|Alice| = √(25 + 16 + 1) = √42 ≈ 6.48.  |Bob| = √(25 + 25 + 4) = √54 ≈ 7.35.
3

Cosine similarity: divide

Dividing by both lengths removes how generous each person is with stars and keeps only the direction of their taste. Use your results from steps 1 and 2.

cos(Alice, Bob) = ÷ ( × ) = ?
47 ÷ (6.48 × 7.35) = 47 ÷ 47.63 ≈ 0.99. Close to 1 means Alice and Bob point in almost the same direction.

Content-based filtering: more of what Alice already likes

Now ignore Bob, Cara and Dave completely. Describe each movie by its features, build a profile of Alice's taste, and compare.

1

Describe every movie

Build Alice's taste profile

Weight each movie's features by Alice's rating, then divide by the total of her ratings (5 + 4 + 1 = 10). The ratings are already filled in; add each movie's feature value.

Click a box, then click a number in the feature table above. Or type it.

Action = ( 5 × + 4 × + 1 × ) ÷ 10 = ?
Romance = ( 5 × + 4 × + 1 × ) ÷ 10 = ?

In order: Mad Max, John Wick, The Notebook.

Action: (5 × 1.0 + 4 × 0.9 + 1 × 0.0) ÷ 10 = 8.6 ÷ 10 = 0.86. Romance: (5 × 0.1 + 4 × 0.2 + 1 × 1.0) ÷ 10 = 2.3 ÷ 10 = 0.23. Mad Max counts five times and The Notebook once, so the profile leans hard toward action.
3

Score the unseen movies

Cosine between Alice's profile [0.86, 0.23] and each unseen movie. The lengths are filled in for you; add the movie's action and romance values.

Click a box, then click a number in the feature table above. Or type it.

Titanic = ( 0.86 × + 0.23 × ) ÷ ( 0.89 × 0.95 ) = ?
Top Gun: (0.86 × 0.8 + 0.23 × 0.4) ÷ (0.89 × 0.89) = 0.78 ÷ 0.80 ≈ 0.98. Titanic: (0.86 × 0.3 + 0.23 × 0.9) ÷ (0.89 × 0.95) = 0.47 ÷ 0.84 ≈ 0.55. Same answer as collaborative filtering: recommend Top Gun, without looking at any other user.
